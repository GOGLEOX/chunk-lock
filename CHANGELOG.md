# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - Public Testing

Initial public testing build.

### Added

- Persistent chunk claim storage.
- Item-based claiming with `minecraft:map` by default.
- Optional `chunklock:claim_stake` item.
- Claim/unclaim/info/list/admin commands.
- Block break, block place, container, and bucket protection.
- Optional explosion and mob griefing protection.
- Claim limits and dimension claim controls.
- Optional JourneyMap overlay foundation.
- Internal client claim sync provider for future map integrations.

### Known Limitations

- No teams.
- No GUI.
- No chunk loading.
- No economy.
- JourneyMap support is optional.
- Xaero support is not implemented.
- FTB Chunks support is limited to optional self-disable behavior.
